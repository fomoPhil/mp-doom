# MP DOOM 1.0.4

Released November 8, 2026.

- Fixes DOOM not starting on SD cards with a space or symbol in their name. On 1.0.3 those cards went straight to the normal MPC screen. Any exFAT card name now works.
- MAIN VOLUME goes 25% louder at the top of the knob.
- Everything else matches 1.0.3: the controls, music, saves, SD card layout and the Windows and Mac setup.

If you installed 1.0.3, replace the whole `autorun` folder. Your saves stay in `MPC-DOOM/saves`.

Phil confirmed that the 1.0.4 starter booted into DOOM from an exFAT card named `MP DOOM`, a name 1.0.3 refused, then saved and returned to the MPC screen. See `VERIFICATION.md` for the recorded results and remaining checks.
